# ERC13 Operational Gates

ERC13 is Project Enclave's internal evidence and editorial governance protocol.

The current implementation includes Gates 1 through 8.

## G1 — Source lineage

Every publishable claim must retain traceable source identifiers.

Purpose:
- prevent orphaned claims,
- preserve provenance,
- support later revision and audit.

## G2 — Evidence classification

Every claim must be explicitly classified.

Current categories include:
- observed,
- derived,
- contextual,
- modeled,
- hypothesis,
- unresolved.

Purpose:
- prevent unlike evidence types from being presented as equivalent.

## G3 — Causality boundary

Observed accounting facts cannot be promoted into causal claims without additional evidence.

Purpose:
- prevent chronology from being mistaken for causation.

## G4 — Source quality

Source tier and assigned confidence must be coherent.

Examples:
- primary government sources may support high confidence that an event occurred,
- unregistered or weak sources cannot receive high confidence without corroboration.

Purpose:
- prevent confidence from outrunning source quality.

## G5 — Uncertainty and revision

Contextual evidence must preserve uncertainty and revision history.

Purpose:
- prevent silent replacement of earlier interpretations,
- preserve the ability to correct the record.

## G6 — Unresolved knowledge integrity

Unknowns must be structured rather than vague.

An unresolved item identifies:
- the open question,
- why it is unresolved,
- linked evidence,
- whether it can be resolved,
- what evidence or condition could resolve it.

Purpose:
- preserve uncertainty when uncertainty is part of the truth.

## G7 — Competing explanations

Candidate explanations may coexist.

A single causal hypothesis cannot be promoted as preferred merely because no alternative was recorded. Preferred hypotheses require:
- an explicit competing explanation,
- evidence-based rationale,
- exposed assumptions,
- disconfirming evidence criteria.

Purpose:
- reduce premature narrative closure and confirmation bias.

## G8 — Materiality

Context does not become important to the debt movement merely because it is dramatic, recent, or politically salient.

Materiality requires evidence.

A context item may be classified as material only when:
- supporting evidence exists,
- magnitude is understood where relevant,
- a direct accounting link is known.

Otherwise, materiality remains possibly material, immaterial, or unknown.

Purpose:
- prevent salience from being mistaken for explanatory importance.

## Governing principles

**Act when action is necessary, but never surrender the capacity to doubt the model that justified the action.**

**Uncertainty should be preserved when uncertainty is itself part of the truth.**

ERC13 is intended to function as an editorial immune system: it does not prevent interpretation, but it attempts to stop unsupported certainty from passing into public output as established knowledge.
